# Code of conduct

Keep discussions respectful, specific and focused on the work. Welcome questions and differing technical interpretations. Critique evidence and methods without attacking contributors.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, disclosure of private information and deliberate fabrication of research evidence are unacceptable. Do not share sensitive reviewer or participant information without permission.

Repository maintainers may moderate discussions, request corrections, remove inappropriate content or restrict participation. When reporting a concern, use a contact method published by the repository owner; do not disclose private details in a public issue. This document does not promise a dedicated reporting team or response deadline.

These expectations apply to repository issues, pull requests and project-related discussions.
